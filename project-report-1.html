International Journalism Training Program

UNC Hussman School of Journalism and Media | 2026

Illustrative cover image created for this portfolio.

Project Overview

In September 2026, UNC Hussman hosted a five-day professional training program for Korean journalists. The program combined academic workshops with visits to local media organizations, providing opportunities for professional learning and international exchange.

My Role

I coordinated program logistics, communicated with speakers and participating organizations, arranged media visits, and supported participants throughout the program. My goal was to create a well-organized and meaningful learning experience.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my skills in program management, cross-cultural communication, and collaboration. It also reminded me how valuable experiential learning can be in connecting professionals from different countries and backgrounds.
