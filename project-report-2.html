Project Overview

The Visiting Scholar Program at UNC Hussman provides opportunities for international scholars to engage in academic research, professional exchange, and collaboration with faculty and the university community. The program supports scholars as they navigate a new academic and cultural environment.

My Role

I serve as a primary point of contact for international visiting scholars, helping coordinate their arrival, orientation, and ongoing support. I communicate with faculty members, university offices, and scholars to address questions, coordinate program arrangements, and connect visitors with relevant campus resources.

What I Learned

Working with visiting scholars has strengthened my ability to communicate across cultures, coordinate with different university departments, and respond to individual needs. I have learned that clear communication, thoughtful preparation, and timely support can make a significant difference in a scholar's experience.
